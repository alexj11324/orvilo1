# Sign-In Moves to the Accounts Portal

Sign-in is now owned entirely by the accounts portal at
accounts.aspectlylabs.com. The app's own sign-in, sign-up, verify-email, and
reset-password pages forward to the portal instead of rendering local forms,
and signing out ends the portal session too.

Once you are signed in on the portal, the app exchanges the portal's Clerk
session for an Orvilo session cookie, so returning to the app lands you
signed in without a second login. Existing sessions keep working until they
expire; afterwards you simply sign in once on the portal.

Password and single-sign-on management now live on the portal as well — the
Settings rows for password and connected accounts describe that instead of
offering local controls. Desktop and CLI authorization flows are unchanged.

## Improvements and fixes

- Sign-out now lands on the portal and ends the Clerk session, instead of
  dropping only the app's local session cookie.
