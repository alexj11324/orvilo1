# Team Resources, Draggable Favorites, and Honest Display Options

Team Home's Documents tab is no longer a placeholder. Members can create team Pages, attach links, organize them into sections, and open the same list after a reload — all scoped to the team, with workspace isolation and access checked on every read and write. Pages created by a member who later leaves stay available to the team, including their history.

Favorites in the sidebar reorder the way you would expect: drag a row with the pointer, or use the keyboard and the Move up / Move down menu items — all three share one ordering contract. Releasing a drag does not navigate away, the new order persists across reloads, and a conflicting change refreshes the list with feedback instead of silently failing.

The Projects display-options popover now follows a real capability matrix. Every option you can enable actually changes the list — layout, grouping, ordering, and per-property columns — and persists for you personally. Options that cannot work are disabled with the reason on a tooltip, so nothing looks clickable while doing nothing.

## Improvements and fixes

- **Deleting a team Page removes it from Team Home immediately**, without a manual refresh.
- **The project detail and list controls match the Linear reference**, including header-only ordering for the properties that support it.
- **The sidebar stays open during navigation**, and the desktop nav panel no longer collapses on drag interactions.
- **Workspace document search respects team Page access**, so team Pages surface to members and stay hidden from outsiders.
